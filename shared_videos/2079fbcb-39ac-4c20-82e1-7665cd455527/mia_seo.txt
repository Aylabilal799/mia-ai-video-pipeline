LONG DESCRIPTION
I almost missed my train this morning because I completely overslept. Sprinted through the station with my bag half zipped and somehow made it with ten seconds to spare. Once I sat down I just started laughing at myself for how ridiculous that was. The city rolled past the window and for a while I just watched it in silence. Got off a few stops early and wandered into a street market I'd never noticed before. Ended up buying a tiny ceramic bowl from an old vendor who barely spoke but smiled the whole time we talked. Sometimes the mornings that start in chaos turn into the best ones.
